Process

How does it work?

Three simple steps from symptoms to a full health report.

Step 01
Enter Symptoms

Select from 132 clinically validated symptoms. Add up to 5 for the most accurate prediction from our ML model.

Step 02
AI Prediction

Our Support Vector Classifier trained on thousands of cases maps your symptoms to the most likely diagnosis instantly.

Step 03
Full Health Report

Get disease info, precautions, medications, tailored diets, and workout plans — all in one place.
