Our Story

About PulseAI

Where health meets technology for a brighter, healthier future.

🏥

Welcome to PulseAI

We are a dedicated team of healthcare professionals, data scientists, and technology enthusiasts with a common goal — make healthcare accessible, understandable, and personalized for everyone.

👁️

Our Vision

We envision a world where access to healthcare information is not just a luxury but a fundamental right. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful idea: empower individuals with knowledge and tools to take control of their health.

👥

Who We Are

A dedicated team of healthcare professionals, data scientists, and technology enthusiasts with extensive experience in both healthcare and cutting-edge AI technology.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a seamless and intuitive platform that harnesses AI and machine learning to identify potential health concerns based on your reported symptoms, while offering a wealth of educational resources.

⚙️

How We Do It

Our platform uses a robust SVC machine learning model trained on a vast dataset of symptoms and diseases. By inputting your symptoms, our system generates accurate predictions about potential illnesses.

❤️

Your Well-being, Our Priority

Your health is our top priority. We provide not only accurate predictions but also comprehensive information — descriptions, precautions, medications, dietary advice, and workout tips.

Technology

Built with precision

Python & Flask

Backend powered by Python with Flask framework for seamless API routing and ML model serving.

Scikit-learn SVC

Support Vector Classifier trained on the Kaggle Medicine Recommendation Dataset with 98% accuracy.

Pandas & NumPy

Data manipulation, preprocessing, and feature engineering handled by Pandas and NumPy pipelines.
